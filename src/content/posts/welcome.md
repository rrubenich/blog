This blog runs on Inkline, an Astro theme built around one idea: the page should disappear while you read. There is one column, one accent color, and almost no JavaScript.

## Start in two minutes

Open `src/config.ts` and change the title and URL. Then add a Markdown file to `src/content/posts/`. That is the whole setup.

```ts
export const SITE = {
  title: "rubenich.dev",
  url: "https://rubenich.dev",
};
```

## What you get

Posts support Markdown and MDX, tags, a contents list, search, RSS, a sitemap, and light and dark themes. Open any post and tap **Aa** in the header to change text size, width, and typeface. Your choice is remembered on this device.

## What you don't get

There are no author pages, share buttons, or comment widgets. Each of those is easy to add, and easier to live without.
